# ClosetDay Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026.

Publisher: Dmytro Polokhalo. Contact: [dmytro@nycintel.app](mailto:dmytro@nycintel.app).

ClosetDay lets you build a personal wardrobe and packing lists. It does not require a ClosetDay account and does not include advertising or third-party analytics.

## Photos and wardrobe information

Garment photos, original images, wardrobe details, packing lists, preferences, and cached destination forecasts are stored in the app on your device. On iPhone and iPad, background removal, photo cleanup, item classification and color suggestions happen on-device using a bundled model. The Android version asks you to review optional item details and does not upload photos for recognition. ClosetDay does not upload garment photos to an AI service or a ClosetDay server.

You control camera permission and the photos you select with the system photo picker. You can remove saved pieces and trips in the app. Device backups managed by Apple or Google may include app data according to your device settings.

## Backups you choose to share

On iPhone or iPad, when you export or share a wardrobe backup, the archive contains wardrobe information and garment photos, including retained originals. The Android version can import a backup that you select from Files. Any destination or person you choose to share an exported backup with receives that information. Deleting an item in ClosetDay does not remove copies you previously exported.

## Destination search and weather

On iPhone and iPad, city searches use Apple MapKit and Apple WeatherKit receives the coordinates of your selected destination to provide forecasts. On Android 0.1.3 and later, city suggestions use a bundled GeoNames directory. If there is no local match, the Android system geocoder receives the entered search. City-sized coordinates, rounded to two decimal places, are sent to MET Norway to obtain forecasts. MET Norway also receives the network IP address when processing the request. Older Android closed-test builds use Open-Meteo for city search and forecasts. These travel features do not request your current GPS location. Weather attribution appears within forecasts.

## Purchases and ratings

On iPhone and iPad, Apple handles purchases, restoration, payment details, and App Store ratings. ClosetDay checks Apple's signed purchase records on the device to determine whether the one-time unlock is owned. On Android 0.1.3 and later, Google Play handles purchases and restoration. ClosetDay verifies Google Play’s signed purchase records on the device to determine whether the one-time unlock is owned. Signed records are stored locally for offline access and excluded from Android backup and device transfer. Older Android closed-test builds do not offer purchases. ClosetDay does not receive your payment card details. Review-request milestones are stored locally to avoid repeatedly asking for a rating.

## Support

In-app bug reports use Formspree to receive the message you explicitly enter, with a static ClosetDay support subject. This form does not attach photos, wardrobe records, device details, logs, or your email address. Formspree processes the submission and associated network information, including the submitting IP address, to deliver reports and protect the service against spam and abuse under its [privacy policy](https://formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy/). Reports are delivered to the publisher’s existing shared support inbox. The app displays whether a submission was accepted or could not be confirmed.

If you email support, we receive the email address, message, and any attachments you choose to provide. We use that information to answer your request and investigate the issue. Do not send sensitive information unnecessarily. You may request deletion of support correspondence by emailing us, subject to any records we must retain by law. Public GitHub issues are visible to other people; use email for private questions.
